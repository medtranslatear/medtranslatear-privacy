Features

understand your meds.

12 Languages

Clear summaries in English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Dari, Farsi, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Korean, Portuguese, and German.

📷

Three Ways to Scan

Scan a barcode, snap a photo of the label, or just type the medication name. Whatever's easiest.

Adjustable Reading Level

Choose between Grade 5 and Grade 8 summaries so the explanation fits your needs, not the other way around.

Every result can be read to you out loud — great for low vision, hands-free situations, or just easier listening.

Star important medications and access your full scan history anytime, without needing an account.
