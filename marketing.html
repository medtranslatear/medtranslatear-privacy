FAQ

Common questions

What is MedTranslate AR?

MedTranslate AR is a free app that scans medication labels and barcodes, then translates and simplifies the instructions into plain language in the patient's own language.

What is the AI actually reading?

It reads the printed label or barcode using computer vision and OCR, then rewrites the clinical text — dosage, timing, and warnings — into one plain instruction, reviewed against pharmacist-verified templates.

How is this different from a translation app?

A translation app converts words. MedTranslate AR simplifies meaning — turning clinical phrasing into an instruction a patient can act on, not just a literal translation of it.

Is this a substitute for pharmacist counseling?

No. It's a second layer of clarity between the pharmacy counter and home — patients should still discuss questions with their pharmacist or care team.

What happens to my data?

Scanned label data stays on-device by default and is never sold. Health systems only see aggregate, de-identified reporting if they opt into a partnership.

Which languages are supported?

Over 30 languages today, with translation and simplification quality reviewed on an ongoing basis.

How do I get it?

Download it free from the App Store — no account or clinical setup required to start scanning.

Safety & privacy
