How It Works

Three steps to clarity.

No setup, no account, no medical knowledge required. Just scan and understand.

1

Scan Your Medication

Point your camera at a barcode, snap a photo of the label, or type the medication name directly.

2

Get a Plain-Language Summary

Instantly see what the medication is for, how to take it, and what to watch out for — no jargon.

3

Listen in Your Language

Tap the speaker to have your summary read aloud, in whichever of the 12 supported languages you prefer.
