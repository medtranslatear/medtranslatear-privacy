5How the App Works

5.1 The App uses your device's camera to scan barcodes and text on medication packaging and prescription labels.
5.2 Scanned text is sent to a third-party AI service (Groq, Inc.) for processing and translation. By using the App, you consent to this data transmission.
5.3 The App does not store any prescription images or text on external servers. All scan history and saved items are stored locally on your device only.
5.4 AI translations are generated using large language models and may not be 100% accurate. Always verify important medical information with your pharmacist.
